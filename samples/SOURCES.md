# Sample sources

Real human writing as style exemplars. Each file has YAML header with source + license.

academic — PLOS OA via api.plos.org, CC BY 4.0 (attribution; DOI in file)  
narrative — Gutenberg *Modern Essays* (38280), PD US  
fun — Gutenberg *Best American Humorous Short Stories* (10947); Twain *1601* (3190); NUS SMS excerpts — PD US + SMS research attribution  
conversational — Chesterfield *Letters to His Son* (Gutenberg 3361); NUS SMS Corpus (English) — PD US; NUS free for research, excerpts only  
email — Enron Email Dataset (CMU / FERC public), PD research corpus; ~30 messages across employees

Not included on purpose: copyrighted modern mag/fashion; full NUS or Enron republish (excerpts only); any living person's private mail outside a public corpus.
