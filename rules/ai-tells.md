# AI tells

Walk the draft. Quote offender, name tell, fix. Structural first, vocab last.

## Structural

1. Uniform sentence length (15–25 word metronome) → shatter the meter
2. Rule-of-three tricolon → two, four, or a sentence
3. Negative parallelism ("not just X, it's Y") → state the claim
4. Signpost framing (Here's the thing, What stuck with me) → lead with the thing
4b. Interest-bridge/label — kill: gets interesting, weirdly interesting, here's where it gets real, the part that actually matters. Delete label, keep claim/scene
4c. "The part/section/bit X" pointer — kill: the part people see, the section that matters, the bit where… State without meta-pointer
4d. Definitional punch — kill: "That joining is fusion." "This is gravity." Name inside a full ordinary sentence
4e. Fake-plain gloss — shows up as heat and light, daylight is what falls out, comes out as energy → becomes / is released as / you get
5. Hollow aphoristic closer ("In the end, the data doesn't lie") → end on a detail
5b. False humility closer (no deep lesson / maybe no big takeaway then tidy moral) → cut the bow
6. Appositive piling → one or none
7. Over-smooth transitions (Moreover / Furthermore / Additionally)
8. Every para same shape (claim, elaborate, hedge, upbeat resolve)
9. Hedged-to-center neutrality
10. Reassurance pair (two tidy pre-rebuttals)
11. Self-posed rhetorical question ("The result? A complete rethink.")
12. False range ("from innovation to cultural transformation")
13. Fractal summary (intro previews, body says, conclusion retells)
14. Missing mess (zero asides, frictionless glass)
15. Grandiose stakes inflation
16. Listicle in a trench coat (First… Second… Third… as prose)

## Performance (trying too hard to sound human)

P1 constructed antithesis for show  
P2 telegraphic fragment-stacking ("Brutal. Labor thinning. Floor by 2026.")  
P3 inserted quirky aside (charm bait)  
P4 one-word dramatic sentence alone  
P5 mannered aphorism / kicker closer  
P6 punchy-for-effect a normal person wouldn't type  
P7 default short declarative openers — bare 4–8 word S–V–O hooks as para/piece openers. Hitch / mid-action / mess / longer line. Cap density; never consecutive openers  
P8 over-clever workshop metaphor (soft) — lawns winning, jokes aimed at someone, nuclei brief meetings, force wins every time. Plain what happened. No metaphor banlist; cut when it smells like an exercise  
P9 weird verb-as-noun reification (soft) — that squeeze, the shove, the joining. Use normal verb or standard noun  
P10 faux-casual science (soft) — hydrogen's kind, not kitchen chemistry, not tabletop physics  
P11 meta teach-back cosplay (soft; often hard-kill in explainers) — if that still feels thin, the usual gaps are, if you try teaching it back without notes. Drop method commentary; keep explanation  

If it sounds like a copywriting class, be suspicious.

## Product / content tells (modern agents pass banlists)

- Generic-vivid detail (pasta jars, forty yards, twelve feet closer) — user grit or plain
- Stock internet bits (cart-return morality, parking territoriality, closer-spot hunting) — only if user cares; never invent as flavor
- Simulated first-person without cost ("I have definitely had moments where…") — their moments or stay careful second/third
- Sealed observational kit: hook → contrast → vignettes → role-flip → soft sociology. Break it; leave a loop open
- Multi-beat sealed assist (email/chat): reframe + expand + redefine + resource pitch + justify. Send the one useful point

## Register (email, DM, short ask)

17. soft padded setup ("I've been trying to understand how…")  
18. high-construal abstraction they must translate into their week  
19. essayist stuck-point staging before the real question  
20. mini scene-setting in cold short prose  
21. writerly compounds / soft metaphors where plain works  
22. performed humility ("I'd value your perspective")  
23. dual-option CTA  
24. dual asks in a row  
25. template soft-out ("Totally understand if you're swamped")  
26. unfinished intimacy (personal detail no payoff)  
27. over-deprecation ("half-baked takes") undercutting the ask  
28. uniform smooth register end-to-end  

Zero banned words can still read AI. Re-read as if you typed it on your phone to a real person.

## Email / short anti-templates

If it could appear in 10k cold/polite emails, rewrite the actual thought:

Hope this finds you well · I came across [your X] · I'm reaching out because · I noticed [Company] recently… · caught my attention / eye · I'd love to (opener) · I'm passionate about / thrilled to · from your seat / where you sit · I'm not trying to sell you · resonate / shaped how I think · what that actually looks like in practice · the real bottleneck (and other "the real X") · move the needle, low-hanging fruit, circle back, double-click, skin in the game

Sounds "professional but warm" → AI. Plain words for the real thought.

Don't recite their bio back at them. Personalization = your honest reason for writing, not their LinkedIn read aloud.

## Vocab smell (rotates; not the foundation)

delve, tapestry, testament, landscape (abstract), realm, robust, seamless, cutting-edge, leverage (for use), harness, navigate (metaphor), foster, empower, elevate, unleash, ecosystem (abstract), journey (abstract), paradigm, pivotal, underscore(s), highlight (verb padding), meticulous, holistic, synergy, utilize, game-changer, nestled, vibrant, thriving, unpack, deep dive

Filler hedges: it is important to note, it's worth noting, moreover, furthermore, in today's [X], as we move forward, only time will tell

Chatbot: I hope this helps!, Great question!, Certainly!, Let me know if you need anything else

## Em dash (external)

Avoid — and en-dashes as separators and ` -- `. Commas or periods. Ranges: "1 to 4" not "1–4" if the host mangles dashes.
